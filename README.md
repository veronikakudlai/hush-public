## Contact

**Technical support:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/veronikakudlai/hush-public/issues)  
**Private privacy inquiries:** [privacy_policy.ios@proton.me](mailto:privacy_policy.ios@proton.me)

Issues are public. Never include personal health information, private records or other sensitive data in an issue. Send private questions to the email above.

These policies describe data handling by the HUSH app. They do not govern third-party services such as device backups or app marketplace operations.
