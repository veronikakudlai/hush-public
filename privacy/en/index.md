# HUSH Privacy Policy

**Privacy contact:** privacy_policy.ios@proton.me

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information is used by the HUSH mobile application (the “App”), where it is stored, and how you can manage it.

We respect your privacy and aim to make the App available without requiring you to share personal information with the developer. HUSH helps you maintain a personal record of headache episodes, review your own observations, and use breathing, relaxation, and sound practices.

You decide what information to enter. Optional fields are not required for the App’s main functions.

### 2. Information you may save

Depending on how you use HUSH, the App may store:

- a display name you choose for your profile;
- headache episode details, such as time, place, pain location, type, and intensity;
- symptoms and circumstances that you choose to associate with an episode;
- blood pressure and pulse measurements, if you enter them;
- personal notes and information about how an episode ended;
- preferences, including language and display settings.

Some of this information may relate to your health. It is used to display your records, history, and neutral summaries within the App.

HUSH is not intended for storing identity documents, payment details, or unrelated sensitive information. We recommend that you do not enter such details into free-text fields.

### 3. Where your information is stored

In the current version of HUSH, your entries and preferences are stored locally on your device. You do not need to create a HUSH account or send your entries to a developer-operated server to use these features.

Using the App does not, by itself, give the developer remote access to your personal records. Information saved in HUSH is accessed through the App on the device where it was created.

The security and availability of locally stored information also depend on your device and operating-system settings. If device backup is enabled, App data may be included in backups handled by your chosen provider. Such backups are subject to that provider’s terms and your device settings.

### 4. How information is used

The App uses your information to provide the functions you choose: saving and displaying entries, resuming an unfinished episode, reviewing history, producing neutral statistics, exporting data, and applying preferences.

The App does not use your entries to diagnose a condition, establish the cause of a headache, prescribe treatment, or provide medical recommendations.

The current version does not provide user registration or history synchronization through a HUSH account.

### 5. Sharing with third parties

The current version of HUSH does not automatically send information you enter to the developer for storage or analysis, sell user records to third parties, or use those records for personalized advertising.

If you choose to export or share a file with another person, application, or cloud service, the recipient’s practices will govern any subsequent handling of that file. Before sharing, please review its contents and make sure you are comfortable disclosing the information it includes.

App installation, updates, and operation of the mobile platform may involve technical information processed independently by your app marketplace or device provider under its own privacy terms. This Policy does not govern those independent services.

### 6. Exporting and deleting your information

HUSH allows you to export completed episode records into a file for your personal use. You decide whether and where to save or share that file.

The data management section allows you to delete completed episode history or all saved episodes. Deleting episodes does not necessarily delete profile preferences, which are stored separately from episode history.

Deleting the App from your device will generally remove its local app data. Previously exported files and device backups may remain outside the App and must be managed using the applicable device or service controls.

### 7. Keeping information secure

We aim to limit the information handled by HUSH to what is needed for its features and avoid requesting personal details that are not necessary to use the App.

Because your entries are stored on your device, we recommend using its available security protections, including a passcode, biometric access controls, current operating-system updates, and care when sharing exported files.

No storage method can eliminate every risk of unauthorized access, including risks arising from a lost or compromised device.

### 8. Health information and intended use

HUSH is intended for personal well-being records and self-guided relaxation practices. It is not a medical diagnostic, treatment, or prevention tool and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Your records and summaries reflect information you entered and should not be considered medical conclusions. Seek professional medical assistance if your condition worsens or you experience unusual or severe symptoms.

### 9. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy if App features, information practices, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be available at the public link listed on the HUSH app-store page.

Where required by law or platform rules, we will provide appropriate notice of material changes to how user information is handled.
